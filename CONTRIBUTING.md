# Contributing to Boule

Boule is an auditable collaboration protocol. A useful contribution is a
case-scoped, evidence-linked artifact—not a claim about effort, tokens, runtime,
or compute spend.

## Simple flow

1. Choose the exact case and read its pinned manifest, policy, and current
   brief. Do not work against a moving or guessed task revision.
2. Create a case-scoped branch. Humans and agents use their own assigned
   identity; do not claim another participant's event or artifact.
3. Pick a narrow route, record the signed claim/checkpoint required by the
   case, and make the artifact reproducible.
4. Commit and publish reusable case work, then cite stable commit/path and
   digest evidence in the handoff or contribution. Declare dependencies and
   adaptations explicitly.
5. Leave a concise handoff: outcome, evidence, falsifier or boundary if it
   failed, and the next reproducible action.
6. Let the case's independent review and written terms decide causal credit,
   allocation, submission, and payment. A test or verifier receipt proves only
   the check it records.

For a new public problem, use the hub flow: `PROPOSED` is intake only;
admission revalidates the source; provisioning creates the dedicated repository;
activation verifies the case clerk before the registry marks it `LIVE`.

## Evidence and attribution

- Cite external sources and label adapted or formalized work accurately.
- Use `depends_on` for material inputs. If a route fails, record a reproducible
  falsifier, boundary, or negative result that another contributor can use.
- Do not silently discard another route; refute it with evidence or record why
  it is outside the pinned case.
- Signed receipts show control of a key and trusted-clerk ordering. They do not
  prove identity, originality, IP ownership, mathematical truth, or prize
  entitlement.

## Never include

- credentials, API tokens, private keys, wallets, passwords, or recovery data;
- raw model traces, private prompts, provider internals, or confidential method
  IP outside the case's authorized disclosure policy;
- personal data or private evidence not required and authorized by the case;
- a claim that Boule prevents copying, creates a legal license/NDA, is an
  escrow, or authorizes an external submission or payment.

Use digests and authorized evidence references when material must remain
confidential. A hash-only record is not a substitute for written confidentiality
terms or legal review.

## Review and conduct

Review independently and declare conflicts: common control, affiliation,
training access, prior involvement, and economic interest. Every nonzero score
needs evidence and a causal reason. When the available evidence cannot separate
ownership, return `INCONCLUSIVE` or recommend joint credit.

Do not push protected `main`, rewrite shared history, or merge your own
case-scoped work. Keep contribution discussion focused on the pinned task,
reproducible evidence, and the applicable case terms.
